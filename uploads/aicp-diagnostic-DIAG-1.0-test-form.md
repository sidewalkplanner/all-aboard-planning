# AICP Domain Diagnostic — Test Form DIAG-1.0

**100 questions | approximately 2 hours | one scored attempt**

## Directions

Each question or incomplete statement is followed by four suggested answers or completions. Select the single best response. You may move forward and backward, flag items for review, and leave items blank. Unanswered items are scored as incorrect. There is no penalty for guessing. A basic calculator is permitted; items requiring one are marked **[calculator]**.

Some questions refer to a scenario or an exhibit that precedes them. Scenario questions are grouped and must be answered in order.

This diagnostic is designed to show you **where to study**, not to predict a pass or fail. Answer as you would on exam day rather than looking anything up — an inflated score here costs you preparation time later.

If confidence tagging is enabled, mark each answer **Confident** or **Unsure**. Items you answered confidently but incorrectly are the highest-value items in your report.
