# AICP Practice Exam 1

**Blueprint Prep · Full-Length Practice Exam**

170 questions · 3.5-hour time limit · aligned to the APA AICP Certification Exam Content Outline (updated December 2021, nine domains)

---

## How to use this exam

Set a timer for **3 hours 30 minutes** and work straight through all 170 questions, as you would on exam day. Mark any question you guess on. Score yourself with the Answer Key (Part II), then use the Diagnostic (Part III) to see which of the nine content domains are weakest and where to focus.

This exam is weighted to match the real exam's nine domains:

| Domain | Questions | Numbers |
|---|---|---|
| Fundamental Planning Knowledge (15%) | 26 | 1–26 |
| Plan & Policy Development (15%) | 25 | 27–51 |
| Communication & Interaction (13%) | 22 | 52–73 |
| Plan Implementation (12%) | 20 | 74–93 |
| Areas of Practice (12%) | 21 | 94–114 |
| Research & Assessment Methods (11%) | 19 | 115–133 |
| Code of Ethics & Professional Conduct (10%) | 17 | 134–150 |
| Administration & Management (6%) | 10 | 151–160 |
| Leadership (6%) | 10 | 161–170 |

Several questions use the **combination format** (options I, II, III, IV, with answer choices that combine them) and several come in **scenario clusters** of 3–5 linked questions, exactly as the real exam does. Choose the *best* answer, from a national planning perspective, even when more than one option is defensible.
